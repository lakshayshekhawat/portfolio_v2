## Context

Customers' automations bid on loads using business rules: which lanes to take, what to bid, when to skip. Those rules change often, with the market, the season or a customer's own capacity.

## The problem

Every rule change was a support ticket. A customer wrote in, we translated the request into the automation's decision logic, tested it and shipped it. It took days, and the team spent its time on tickets instead of building.

## The insight

Looking across the ticket queue, the same request kept coming back: change my bidding rule, update my pricing. The customers already knew exactly what they wanted. They just had no way to do it themselves.

## What I shipped

- **A self-service rules tool.** A plain-English wizard where customers set and test their own business rules, replacing complex decision-tree logic that only our team could edit.
- **Structured feedback loops.** Regular input from customers, turned into a prioritised improvement backlog.

## The result

- Rule changes went from **days to real time**.
- Support tickets dropped **40%**.
- Together with the feedback loops, customer retention rose from **70% to 90%**.
